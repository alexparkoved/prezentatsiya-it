06 / LAUNCH READINESS
A DELAY HAS CONSEQUENCES

The risks
of delay.

Opening the park without a shared
payment system can create extra costs
and make later integration harder.

TEMPORARY WORKAROUNDS.
LASTING COSTS.

POTENTIAL OPERATIONAL IMPACTS
01

Higher operating costs

Paper tickets, more staff and extra
payment points at attractions.

02

Fragmented systems

Independent POS and accounting tools.
Manual reconciliation and consolidation.

03

Slower visitor flow

Multiple queues can slow entry and reduce
the number of guests served at peak times.

04

Greater revenue risk

More cash points can increase errors,
discrepancies and revenue leakage.

05

Limited financial visibility

Harder to see a consolidated view of
sales across the park in real time.

06

Costlier future expansion

Extra equipment, integration and retraining
can raise ownership costs and limit scaling.

One coordinated launch. Fewer duplicate processes and costs.
